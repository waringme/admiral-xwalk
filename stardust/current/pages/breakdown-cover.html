    What's included in Admiral Breakdown Cover?

    No matter which level of cover you choose, these benefits are included as standard when you choose Admiral as your breakdown provider:

    Named Drivers

    We cover the car shown on your Admiral policy, whether you or any of your named drivers are driving.

    Message Service

    Don't worry if you need to let someone know you're running late, we're always happy to pass on a message or two for you.

    Caravan and Trailer Cover

    We recover caravan and trailers up to 8ft 3in wide and 3.5t in weight.

    Lost or broken keys

    If your keys are lost or broken we’ll transport your vehicle to the nearest garage or an alternative destination of your choice, if no further than 10 miles away from the breakdown.

    Misfuel Assist

    We'll either drain the fuel or recover your vehicle to the nearest suitable repairer. You'll also get 10 litres of fuel so you can continue your journey.

    Alternative Transport

    You'll get a hire car (up to 1600cc) for up to three days to allow you to complete your journey.

    Overnight Accommodation

    We’ll arrange overnight accommodation including breakfast for you and your passengers up to £150 per person (£600 total) while your vehicle is being repaired.

    Electric Vehicles

    Includes cover for electric vehicles.

    A video guide to breakdown cover

    European Travels

    European Breakdown Cover

    Includes all the same benefits as our National Cover but with added European assistance.

    If you need European assistance call us on 0044 (0)2920 943 111 and we'll send help to you within the territorial limits of Europe.

    We'll cover the callout fees and mileage charges needed to repair or assist with your car. If it can't be fixed within 48 hours, or by your departure date, we'll arrange for you, your car and passengers to be taken to your home address.

    Alternative Transport Abroad

    If you breakdown within the territorial limits (Europe), we’ll provide a hire car (up to 1600cc) for up to three days, allowing you to continue your trip in the territorial limits (Europe) while your vehicle remains unroadworthy.

    We’ll also pay up to £150 towards the reasonable cost of alternative transport for an insured person to return and collect the repaired vehicle.

    Emergency Overnight Accommodation

    If you breakdown within the territorial limits (Europe) and your car can’t be repaired the same working day and you can’t get to your pre-booked accommodation, we'll arrange overnight accommodation and breakfast for you and your passengers up to £150 per person (£600 in total).

    Did you know?

    Your safety is important to us; we prioritise drivers who have broken down in a vulnerable situation.

    How the breakdown service works

    Once you've added breakdown cover to your insurance, it couldn't be easier to use. When you call for breakdown assistance, you'll be asked for your:

    • Policy number
    • Car registration, make and model

    • Phone number

    Your questions answered

    Can my dog (or other animal) come with me in the patrol vehicle?

    Dogs, and other domestic pets, can join you in the patrol vehicle if they are secured and the attending breakdown mechanic thinks that it is safe to transport the animal.

    If larger animals, such as horses or sheep, have to be transported then alterative, specialised equipment will have to be arranged for additional cost at the customer's expense.
     

    Would a flat tyre be covered by my breakdown policy?

    Our roadside mechanics aims to fix a customer’s vehicle at the roadside and get them back on the road as quickly as possible.

    If a customer has a puncture, but the tyre can be repaired, they’ll attempt to do this at the roadside. If the tyre is unrepairable but the customer has a spare, they’ll change the wheel at the side of the road. 

    Because some vehicles don’t carry a spare tyre these days, our mechanics can also provide you with a universal wheel as a temporary fix to get you mobile again. 

    Universal tyres are supposed to be a short measure until the driver can get a permanent replacement. 

    Depending on the time of day, the patrol vehicle will either follow you to a nearest garage (if within working hours), or home (if outside working hours) to make sure you reach your destination safely.
     

    Will you still provide breakdown cover if I extend my 90 days European cover on my main policy?

    Yes, if you have European breakdown cover on your main policy then it will automatically provide cover for the additional time you extend it by on your main policy. 

    What do I need to know about driving in Europe?

    We will continue to provide breakdown cover through our providers operating with the European Union (EU).

    Always check the latest Government advice before you go.
     

    How do I report a breakdown if I’m hard of hearing?

    Customers with hearing difficulties can contact us by text on 0786 002 7999
