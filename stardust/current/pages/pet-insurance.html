    Why do I need pet insurance?

    Pet insurance helps protect your cat or dog against the unexpected. It covers the cost of vet bills if your pet is injured or becomes ill.

    You also get access to PawSquad – a 24-hour online vet helpline. So, you can get advice from a vet whenever you need it.

    What type of pet insurance do I need?

    Compare our two cover options.

    Lifetime Time Limited

    Lifetime is our most comprehensive pet cover. Lifetime policies cover your pet for accidents and illnesses up to the policy limit you’ve chosen each year. At renewal, the limit resets.

    It’s best if you want long-term cover.

    Our Lifetime policy includes:

    • Up to £10,000 in vet fee cover
    • Up to £2,000,000 in third-party liability (dogs only)
    • Up to £2,000 in emergency holiday cancellation cover
    • Behavioural, dental and emergency kennel/cattery cover

    Read more about Lifetime cover

    Time Limited covers your pet for accidents and illnesses over 12 months from when the symptoms start.

    When the policy limit is reached or when 12 months have passed (whichever happens first), you will no longer be covered for vet fees for that particular accident or illness.

    It's best if you want short-term cover.

    Our Time Limited policy includes:

    • Up to £4,000 in vet fee cover
    • £1,000,000 in third-party liability (dogs only)
    • Up to £1,500 in emergency holiday cancellation cover
    • Complementary medicine, special diet and emergency kennel/cattery cover

    Read more about Time-Limited cover

    What does pet insurance cover?

    Pet insurance gives you a safety net if your furry friend gets ill or is injured.

    It depends on your level of cover, but you're covered for things like:

    • vet bills for your pet’s accidents and illnesses
    • your pet’s dental care as a result of an accident
    • cancelling your holiday because of an emergency with your pet
    • your pet’s CT/MRI scans
    • your pet’s treatment like physiotherapy or hydrotherapy

    Always check your policy book to understand what you are and aren’t covered for, and to check your limits and exclusions.

    What doesn't pet insurance cover?

    Pet insurance doesn't cover everything. Here's some things we don't cover:

    Pre-existing conditions

    We don't cover any illness or injury that your pet had before your insurance policy starts.

    We define a pre-existing medical condition as something that may have been identified by a vet, be under investigation by a vet, or otherwise known to you before your policy starts. It could be diagnosed or undiagnosed.

    Claims within the waiting period

    A waiting period is the amount of time you must wait after your policy starts before you can make a claim.

    We don't cover any injury that happens within the 24 hours of your policy starting, or any illness your pet gets within the first 14 days.

    Routine health treatment

    We don’t cover any treatments which are considered routine care or any kind of regular treatment that’s needed to keep your pet healthy. This covers things like:

    • vaccination costs
    • flea or parasite control products
    • bathing, grooming, nail clipping or de-matting your pet
    • spaying/castration

    Check your policy book for the full list.

    Breeding or pregnancy

    We don’t cover costs for pregnancy, giving birth, or any medical issue to do with your pet breeding. This applies to both accidental and intentional pregnancies.

    Read more about pet insurance in your policy book for the full list of exclusions.

    How we calculate your pet insurance cost

    There are lots of factors that help us to work out the cost of your insurance.

    Remember, pet insurance being cheap doesn’t mean it’s the best – or right for you and your pet. Always make sure the policy you’re looking at is enough to cover you, your pet and their needs.

    What affects the cost of your pet policy

    Back

    How we calculate your pet insurance cost

    We look at lots of different factors to give you a fair and accurate price each year:

    • Age - like us, our pets are sadly more likely to get ill as they get older. This means they're more likely to see a vet for treatment, so you're more likely to need to make a claim on your insurance. Because of this, the cost of your insurance will go up as your pet gets older.
    • Type of cover - the type of cover you choose will also affect the cost of your insurance. For example, our Lifetime Platinum cover has lots of extra benefits and higher vet fee limits, so it’s more expensive than our lower levels of cover.
    • Breeds - certain breeds tend to have more health problems, which means they can cost more to insure.
    • Location - vet costs can vary across the UK. If vet treatment costs more in your local area, your insurance can cost more too.
    • Claims history - if you’ve made a claim on your policy in the past, it'll affect the price of your pet insurance when it’s time to renew.
    • Insurance Premium Tax - the government decides whether to raise or lower the tax rate, so any changes will affect your price. That means your pet insurance could cost more if the government raises the rate.

      These are some of the most common factors that affect the price of your insurance, but it’s not a full list. There are lots of other details about your pet and your policy that can affect how much you pay.

    Pet insurance FAQs

    Got more questions? Find out more about pet insurance and how it works.

    Does my pet’s age affect pet insurance?

    Yes, your pet’s age can change how much it costs to insure them.

    As your pet gets older, they’re more likely to get ill or injured, which means more trips to the vets and more vet bills. Your pet insurance will go up to cover those costs.

    Do vaccinations affect pet insurance?

    Your pet insurance doesn’t cover vaccinations or boosters – you’ll need to pay for these yourself.

    If you do have pet insurance, make sure your pet gets all their vaccinations and they’re up to date. We don't cover illnesses that vaccinations could help stop.

    Does pet insurance cover the costs of a kennel or cattery?

    Yes, but only in emergency situations. We cover kennel or cattery fees if you're ill or have had an accident and there's no one else to look after your pet.

    How much we cover also depends on the level of cover you have, the time and your cover limits. If you have pet insurance with us, you should check your policy book to see what you are and aren’t covered for.

    Will pet insurance cover life-threatening injuries or illnesses?

    We cover life-threatening injuries and illnesses up to a set limit. This depends on the level of cover you choose.

    Which animals can't be covered by pet insurance?

    We don’t have anything against your pet fish (promise!) - but we only insure dogs and cats.

    What happens if I can’t afford vet bills?

    A vet will usually talk you through the cost of treatment before you agree to anything. Pet insurance is there to help cover the cost of vet bills if your pet is ill or injured.

    The amount you’re covered for depends on the level of cover you choose. Check your policy book to see what you are and aren’t covered for.

    Can I get cover that starts immediately?

    You can get a policy that starts immediately, but waiting periods would apply.

    Our policies cover your pet for illnesses from 14 days after your policy begins. It also covers accidents that happen after the first 24 hours of taking out your policy.

    Why are pedigree animals more expensive to insure?

    Pedigree cats and dogs often have higher insurance costs. This is because they are more likely to have health problems tied to their breed.

    These can be genetic or hereditary conditions, which mean more trips to the vet.

    They also tend to be more expensive to buy, so your insurance could cost more too.

    Policy cover FAQs

    What is excess?

    Excess is an amount you will have to pay towards a claim. With us, all pets under the age of 6 will have a £99 excess. Pets 6 years and over will have an excess of £199.

    You can choose to pay the higher excess of £199 if you have a pet under age 6. We’ll deduct this amount from the claims settlement.

    There is a £250 excess applied once per claim for Third Party Liability cover (dogs only) and third-party accidental damage cover available on our Platinum tier.

    What is a pre-existing condition?

    A pre-existing condition is any illness, injury or complication that results from an injury or illness.

    A pre-existing condition can be diagnosed or undiagnosed. It refers to a medical condition that has been identified or investigated by a vet or is otherwise known to you prior to the start of the insurance.

    A condition is considered pre-existing when it happens within the first 14 days of the policy for illness or 24 hours in the case of injury.

    We don’t cover for pre-existing conditions.

    Will my price change at renewal?

    Your premium may change due to a few factors:

    • Age
    • Breed
    • Location
    • Claims history
    • Medical advances
    • Legislation

    Prices are reviewed annually, and we will let you know your new price, including what you paid last year, as a reference.

    We want to assure you that the renewal price is already at our most competitive rate based on factors like your pet's breed, age and health.

    If you have more than 1 pet, we'll give you 15% off each pet you insure with us on the same policy.

    Do I get discount for adding more pets?

    Absolutely!

    You can get up to 15% off each pet you insure on the same policy that's living in the same household with our MultiPet insurance.
